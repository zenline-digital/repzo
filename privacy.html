Privacy Policy

Operated by ZenLine FZ-LLC (RAKEZ, Ras Al Khaimah, UAE), for THUGFIT. Contact: hello@thugfit.ae · Last updated: 30 October 2026

Who we are: ZenLine FZ-LLC (RAKEZ, UAE), which operates Repzo for THUGFIT. Contact: hello@thugfit.ae.

What we collect: name, email, phone number, country and emirate, and a password stored only as a secure hash. Your training profile (goals, experience, equipment, schedule), body measurements, weight, workout logs, and any health information you choose to enter.

Why we use it: to create your account, build your program, track your progress, send verification and password-reset emails, and provide the service.

Health information: we treat health details as sensitive. We use them only to adjust your program. Do not enter medical details you do not want to share.

Who can see it: you, and THUGFIT staff who need it to run the service. Access is limited and recorded. Hosting, database and email providers process data for us under contract.

Marketing: your email address and phone number are used to send THUGFIT offers and updates. This is accepted automatically when you create your account. You can stop these messages at any time from the Account screen.

Security: passwords are hashed, data is sent over encrypted connections, and access is restricted.

Keeping data: we keep your data while your account is active. When you delete your account, we delete your personal data within 30 days, except records the law requires us to keep.

Your rights: you can ask to see, correct, or download your data, withdraw consent, or delete your account. Email hello@thugfit.ae.

Children: the app is not for anyone under 18.

Law: this policy follows the UAE Personal Data Protection Law (Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021). We will tell you in the app when it changes.

Who processes your data: our database and sign-in service (Supabase), email service (Resend), website hosting (Vercel) and company email (Zoho Mail) handle data on our behalf under written agreements. Data may be stored in data centres outside the UAE, and we only use providers that apply appropriate safeguards.

Health information: health details are used only to build and adjust your training program, and are shown only to the people who run the service.

Automated decisions: your training plan is produced automatically from your answers. You can ask a coach to review it by writing to hello@thugfit.ae.

Breaches: if your personal data is exposed in a way that could harm your rights, we will tell you and the UAE Data Office without undue delay, as the law requires.

Complaints: if you are not satisfied with how we handle your data, you can complain to the UAE Data Office.

Deletion: deleting your account removes your login, profile, consents, weights, workouts, feedback and attachments from our systems within 30 days. Records the law requires us to keep are kept only for the period the law requires.
